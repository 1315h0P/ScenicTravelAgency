About STA

Company Overview

Scenic Travel Agency (STA) is a proudly Papua New Guinean-owned travel management company headquartered in Port Moresby.

Professional & Reliable Travel Solutions

Established to provide professional, reliable, and innovative travel solutions, STA serves corporate organizations, government agencies, development partners, non-government organizations, and individual travellers throughout Papua New Guinea and beyond.

With a strong commitment to service excellence, STA delivers comprehensive travel management solutions tailored to the unique needs of its clients. Through strategic partnerships with airlines, hotels, transport providers, and tourism operators, the company ensures seamless travel experiences while maintaining competitive pricing and high service standards.

Your Journey, Our Commitment

STA's extensive understanding of both domestic and international travel markets enables efficient travel planning, logistical support, and destination management.

Welcome to Scenic Travel Agency (STA). At STA, we believe travel is more than moving people from one destination to another—it is about creating opportunities, building relationships, facilitating business growth, and delivering memorable experiences. As a locally owned Papua New Guinean company, we understand the unique challenges and opportunities within our country and region. Our team is dedicated to providing professional travel solutions that meet the evolving needs of government agencies, corporate organizations, development partners, and individual travellers.

Our success is built on trust, integrity, professionalism, and a commitment to delivering exceptional customer service. We continue to invest in our people, technology, and partnerships to ensure we remain a preferred travel management provider in Papua New Guinea.
